Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 20, 2026

What this app is

LongRaceTranslator is a private tool used only by its developer to manage their own YouTube channels.

Data it accesses

With the account owner's permission (Google OAuth, YouTube scope), the app reads the titles and descriptions of videos on the owner's channels and writes translated titles and descriptions back to those videos.

How data is used and stored

  • Data is used only to add or update video translations on the owner's own channels.
  • Results are kept only in the owner's own Google Sheet. No other server stores them.
  • Data is not sold, shared with third parties, or used for advertising.

The app's use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Revoking access

Access can be removed at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions.
